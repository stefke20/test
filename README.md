## 🍺 Pintje

A tiny web app to drink a virtual pintje. Open `index.html` in a browser (works great on a phone).

- Hold the button (or the space bar) to drink — gulp sounds and rising bubbles included.
- On phones, enable "Kantel je telefoon" and tip your phone toward your face like a glass.
- Finish the glass for a burp, then tap "Nog ene!" to refill.
- Every pour has a 20% chance to be a rare, dark Westvleteren 12 (counted separately).
- Keeps count of today's and all-time pintjes in your browser.
