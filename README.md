## 🍺 Pintje

A web app to drink virtual Belgian beers. Open `index.html` in a browser (works great on a phone).

- **Tap**: hold the button (or the space bar), or tilt your phone, to drink. Finish for a burp and tap "Nog ene!" for the next pour.
- **Rarities**: 21 Belgian beers in six tiers: Gewoon 55%, Ongewoon 25%, Zeldzaam 13%, Episch 5%, Legendarisch 1.99% and the Mythisch *Gouden Pintje van Sint-Arnoldus* at 0.01%.
- **Levels & XP**: every pint drops XP by rarity (10 / 25 / 60 / 200 / 1,000 / 25,000). Each level gives a skill point.
- **Skill tree**: Snelle slokker (drink faster), Bierkenner (more XP), Gelukzak (better odds), Dubbel tappen (pints count double), IJzeren lever (slower zatheidsmeter), Tweede kans (re-roll commons) and the capstone Zegen van Sint-Arnoldus (10× mythic chance).
- **Collectie**: a log of every beer found, plus achievements.
- **Glazen**: unlockable glass skins (tulp, Kwak, trappistenkelk, geuze, bierpul, gouden kelk).
- **Ranglijst**: a shared leaderboard when opened through the claude.ai artifact link; progress is also kept in your browser.
